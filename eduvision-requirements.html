1. Project Overview

EduVision AI is an intelligent learning platform designed to bridge the gap between academic education and the job market in Egypt. The platform uses AI to analyze each student's academic, psychological, and social profile, then provide personalized learning paths.

1.1 Problem Statement

  • Graduates are not adequately prepared for the job market.
  • A gap exists between academic curricula and industry requirements.
  • Personalized learning is missing in conventional systems.
  • Student psychological and social conditions are often ignored.

1.2 Target Users

  • Egyptian university students (primary users).
  • Employees seeking professional development.
  • Instructors and industry experts.
  • Universities and academic institutions.
  • Partner companies for employee training.

5. Constraints & Assumptions

5.1 Constraints

  • Student data must be stored exclusively on Ministry of Communications and IT servers.
  • Each university's access is strictly limited to its own students' data.
  • All instructors must pass a mandatory review by SoftUp before approval.
  • Psychological assessment results are confidential and shared only with the designated counselor.
  • Platform initially targets Egyptian universities before expanding.

5.2 Assumptions

  • Students have reliable access to the internet.
  • Universities have designated psychological counselors available for referrals.
  • Partner companies provide their training needs and requirements.
  • Ministry of Communications and IT provides the necessary hosting infrastructure.
